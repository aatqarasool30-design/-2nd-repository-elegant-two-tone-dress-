
# Design No. 4 - Chocolate & Olive Elegant Dress

A classy and elegant two-tone formal dress design.

![Design](IMG-20261119-WA6642.jpg)

### Design Details:
- Top / Upper Part: Chocolate Brown - Long & Flowy
- Inner Skirt: Olive Green
- Style: Modern, Elegant & Classy
- Look: Messy Bun, Bright & Soft Makeup

### Inspiration:
Chocolate brown adds warmth and depth, while olive green adds a fresh, earthy contrast. This combo gives a rich and premium feel.

Perfect for Formal Events / Parties.
